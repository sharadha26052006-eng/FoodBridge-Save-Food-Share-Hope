🌱 FoodBridge — Save Food, Share Hope

Every Meal Matters. Every Action Counts.

FoodBridge is a social-impact platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting restaurants, hotels, event organisers and households with NGOs and communities in need.

Our mission is simple: ensure that surplus food reaches people who need it instead of going to waste.

---

🌍 The Problem

Every day, perfectly edible food is discarded while millions of people struggle with food insecurity.

Food waste and hunger exist side by side. FoodBridge aims to bridge this gap through a simple, accessible and meaningful digital experience.

💡 Our Solution

FoodBridge creates a connection between:

- 🍽️ Food Donors: Restaurants, hotels, event organisers and households with surplus food.
- 🤝 NGO Partners: Organisations that help redistribute food.
- ❤️ Communities: People and communities who need access to meals.

By making food donation easier, FoodBridge encourages responsible food sharing and community participation.

✨ Key Features

- Easy Food Donation: A simple way to share surplus food.
- NGO Partnerships: Connect food donors with charitable organisations.
- Pickup Coordination: Help coordinate food collection.
- Community Participation: Encourage volunteers and local communities to participate.
- Food Rescue: Give surplus edible food a second chance.
- Impact Tracking: Present meaningful impact goals and illustrative metrics.
